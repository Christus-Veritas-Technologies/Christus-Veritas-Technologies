## Identity
- Light mode, white backgrounds, neon accents used sparingly.
- Gradient #1A2BFF → #0A7BFF → #1CCBFF (bottom to top). Cyan #1CCBFF for the outline, glow and active dot.
- Ink #0A0A0A, steel #4A4A4A. Dark sections #101212 with #DFE0E2 titles and #A0A0A0 body.
- Type: Sora (headlines 800), JetBrains Mono (uppercase labels), Montserrat (wordmark only).
- Motifs: the C mark with its language icons and cyan outline, round icon-pattern discs, neon panels with a glowing white frame, 5-spot tracker, 10-day bar, orange iPhone and purple Galaxy frames.

## Copy rules
Outcomes over features. Find the truth, attach a benefit, make it interesting. For every headline, list what the reader will think, then drill in the good thoughts and tackle the bad ones. Explain anything that looks too good to be true.

Headlines in use: "Look like the biggest business in town." · "Your business on every customer's phone." · "A $1,200 app. Yours for $100." · "From idea to live app in 10 days." · "Software that pays for itself."
